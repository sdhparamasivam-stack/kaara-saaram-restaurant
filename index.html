OUR STORY

Good food, made for memorable moments.

Kaara Saaram Restaurant is a casual South Indian restaurant serving a wide selection of traditional vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes.

Enjoy garden dining, a kids' play area, ample car parking and an AC party hall with seating for up to 100 people.

Approx. ₹200–₹400 per person
